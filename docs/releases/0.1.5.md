# Watt 0.1.5 prerelease

Makes the Back control in tile details easier to click. The arrow, Watt label and surrounding space now share a 112 × 44-point click target, with hover and keyboard-focus feedback. The header height and title placement are preserved.

Includes the dashboard details and per-process network history introduced in 0.1.4. The [0.1.4 prerelease notes](https://github.com/grahamgilbert/watt/releases/tag/v0.1.4) describe collection coverage and remaining limitations.
