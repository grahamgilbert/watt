# Watt 0.1.1 prerelease

This prerelease connects Apple’s per-process Energy Impact collector to Watt’s live dashboard and recorded history.

- Optional, Developer ID-signed privileged helper, installed and removed from Preferences. macOS requires administrator approval in Login Items & Extensions before it runs.
- A configurable Energy Impact popup tile and process scores in Process Explorer, alongside the existing CPU watts and joules.
- Historical Energy Impact averages and peaks use distinct captures and measured capture coverage. Missing, stale, or mismatched process readings remain unavailable.
- Collection follows the sample interval, stops when Watt quits or the Mac sleeps, and resumes for an approved collector after wake or relaunch.
- Both the app and helper support Apple Silicon and Intel Macs, with matching signed identities enforced on their connection.

## Enable Energy Impact

Install Watt in Applications, launch it, and open Preferences. Choose Install collector, then approve Watt in System Settings → General → Login Items & Extensions. Return to Watt; approved collection starts automatically. Preferences shows the current status and allows removing the collector. Add the Energy Impact tile in the layout editor if you already have a saved layout.

## Prerelease limitations

Energy Impact is Apple’s platform-specific relative process score from `powermetrics`, not watts. Watt’s per-process score and app grouping can differ from Activity Monitor’s application totals and sampling window. CPU watts remain a separate CPU-only estimate. Hardware temperatures and system-wide CPU/GPU/SoC power are not included.

Privileged live collection still needs validation on an approved installation. Short-lived or protected processes may not appear in the process table, and recording retains only observed capture intervals. Older history has no Energy Impact readings.
